================================================================================
                    COUNTRY LIFE - Game Description
================================================================================

GENRE:        Open-World Farming & Life Simulation
SETTING:      Rural Vietnamese countryside
ENGINE:       Unity (URP - Universal Render Pipeline)
PERSPECTIVE:  First-person
INPUT:        Unity New Input System (Keyboard + Mouse; touch UI on mobile)
ENDINGS:      2 (Happy and Sad)
LANGUAGE:     Vietnamese / English (toggle in settings)

================================================================================
                          OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Country Life is a first-person open-world farming and life simulation game set
in a rural Vietnamese countryside. You arrive by car at a small village and
must build a life from scratch: clear land, farm a wide variety of crops, build
structures, raise a pet, trade with merchants, fish the river, fight monsters
at night, court Jessica, and complete quests toward two possible endings.

The game couples a complete day/night cycle with persistent farming, a
construction/crafting system, NPC shops and friendship, skills & XP, functional
buildings, enemy encounters, and a story with a happy and a sad ending.

  At Farming level 4: ALL tools cost 25% less stamina.

================================================================================
                          FARMING
================================================================================

FARMING PROCESS:
  1. Use HOE on ground to create a tilled field tile
  2. Select a SEED in inventory
  3. Left-click on the field to plant
  4. Water with WATERING CAN to help growth
  5. Fertilize with FERTILIZER for faster growth (consumes 1 fertilizer)
  6. Wait for crops to reach Stage 4 (fully grown)
  7. Harvest with SCYTHE
  8. Optional: place a WELL to auto-water fields, a FENCE to tank enemy hits,
     or a WATCHTOWER to slow enemies

GROWTH:
  - 4 growth stages total (Stage 0 = empty, Stage 4 = harvestable)
  - Watering and fertilizing speed up growth
  - Each crop has its own seconds-per-stage grow time (see table below)

QUALITY TIERS (Phase 3B) - at maturation a crop can be:
  Normal  (Quality 0): the standard yield.
  Good    (Quality 1): granted by being FERTILIZED.
  Great   (Quality 2): granted by being fertilized AND mostly watered
           (WateredTime coverage >= 60% of its grow time).
  Good harvests have +25% chance for a bonus yield; Great have +50%.

CROP TABLE (grow seconds per stage, ~quality significance):

  Crop         Seed Name       Grow/Stage (s)  Harvest Value   Sell Price
  ----------   -----------     --------------  -------------   ----------
  Wheat        wheat_seed      12              wheat           10 gold
  Corn         corn_seed       14              corn            12 gold
  Potato       potato_seed     13              potato          11 gold
  Carrot       carrot_seed     10              carrot           9 gold
  Tomato       tomato_seed     15              tomato          13 gold
  Strawberry   strawberry_seed 17              strawberry      15 gold
  Pumpkin      pumpkin_seed    16              pumpkin         14 gold
  Onion        onion_seed      11              onion           10 gold
  Sugarcane    sugarcane_seed  13              sugarcane       11 gold
  Rice         rice_seed       12              rice            12 gold

  Full grow time = Stage-4-crop's total: ~40s (carrot) up to ~68s (strawberry).

DAMAGED CROPS (harvested before full maturity or lost mid-growth):
  Each crop has a "damaged_" variant worth ~25-30% of normal price.

SEED PRICES (buy from shops):
  wheat_seed: 3    corn_seed: 4    carrot_seed: 3    tomato_seed: 4
  strawberry_seed: 5    pumpkin_seed: 4    onion_seed: 3
  sugarcane_seed: 4    rice_seed: 3

================================================================================
                          FISHING (RODS & SPECIES)
================================================================================

FISHING PROCESS:
  1. Equip the FISHING ROD
  2. Stand near the river/beach and press/release to cast
  3. When a fish bites, keep the cursor in the green zone while reeling
  4. Reel in before the fish escapes to catch it

Per-species difficulty: each species has its own reel gain, green-zone speed
and flop rate (easier common fish, rarer harder fish).

FISHING ROD UPGRADES (bought at the Fishing Shop):
  Base "Cần Câu" (Fishing Rod) ...... 50 gold
  Level 1 upgrade ................... 40 gold   (+5% reel gain)
  Level 2 upgrade ................... 80 gold   (+10% reel gain)
  Level 3 upgrade .................. 200 gold   (+15% reel gain, -24% flop)
  Upgrade the rod in order (Lv 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3); a rod at a higher level reels
  faster and fish escape less. Max rod level = 3.

FISHING SKILL (XP):
  Each catch grants Fishing XP. Level 2: +20% chance of a double catch.
  Level 4: +15% reel speed. Auto-granted as the Fishing skill levels up.

================================================================================
                          SKILLS & XP (SkillManager)
================================================================================

Two skill tracks, each levels 1-5. Earn XP by doing:
  FARMING  - harvest crops (XP per crop type), use farm tools
  FISHING  - catch fish

Level-ups grant permanent passive bonuses:
  FARMING  L1-5: more efficient harvest/stamina use.
           L2: +25% bonus-crop chance.   L4: all tools -25% stamina.
  FISHING  L1-5: fish escape less, reel faster.
           L2: +20% double-catch chance. L4: +15% reel speed.

Open the Skill panel with the K key to see points/level/perks. Both tracks are
saved with your game.

================================================================================
                          BUILDING SYSTEM
================================================================================

HAMMER TOOL:
  - Left-click in demolish mode: destroy buildings, remove blueprints
  - Press F: open building selection menu
  - Choose a structure from the menu
  - Left-click in place mode: place blueprint (even through existing structures)
  - After placing, automatically returns to demolish mode
  - R/T key: rotate building preview
  - Escape: deselect hammer

BUILDING PROCESS:
  1. Equip HAMMER
  2. Press F to open building menu
  3. Select a building type
  4. Place the blueprint (transparent ghost shows where)
  5. Carry WOOD or STONE resources near the blueprint
  6. Resources auto-deposit when nearby
  7. Building completes when fully supplied

BUILDING TYPES:

WORKSTATIONS (functional buildings placed from the tool palette):
  crafting_stove    - Cooking: steamed rice, chicken rice, fried rice, xoi gac,
                      pumpkin soup (uses rice/meat/egg/pumpkin/salt...)
  preserve_jar      - Preserving: carrot jam, dried fruit, pickled vegetables
  brewing_kettle    - Brewing: rice wine, chili sauce, remedy wine (from demon
                      horns) and tonic (from dark essence + honey)
  well              - Auto-waters every crop in radius each morning
  watchtower        - Slows enemies in radius + shows danger warning
  fence             - Tanky barrier that absorbs enemy hits before they reach you
  coffin / chest    - Storage (two-way bag/chest panel)

Note: Non-floor buildings (except the free-standing chest/well/fence/watchtower
and workstations) require a floor tile underneath to be placed.

================================================================================
                          CRAFTING / COOKING
================================================================================

Craft at workstations (build them with the Hammer, then press E to open the
filtered recipe panel). Recipes combine 2-3 raw ingredients:

  COOKING (crafting_stove):
    com_trang   = rice
    com_ga      = rice + meat
    com_chieu   = com_trang + egg
    xoi_gac     = rice + pumpkin         (+20% stamina regen buff 120s)
    sup_bi_ngo  = pumpkin + salt

  PRESERVES (preserve_jar):
    mut_ca_rot  = carrot + sugar x2
    trai_cay_kho= strawberry + sugar
    dua_chua    = carrot + onion + salt

  BREWING (brewing_kettle):
    ruou_gao    = rice x3 + sugar
    tuong_ot    = tomato x2 + salt + onion
    ruou_tang   = demon_horn + sugar x2
    tinh_duoc   = dark_essence + honey

Food and drink restore stamina/HP when eaten. Crafted goods sell for more than
their raw ingredients, closing the harvest -> craft -> sell loop.

================================================================================
                          WILD MATERIALS & FRAGS
================================================================================

Defeated enemies drop crafting materials:
  demon_horn    - from the Demon King (boss)
  dark_essence  - from bosses/giants

================================================================================
                          PET SYSTEM
================================================================================

TETO - pink-haired companion (scripted intro pet):
  Follows the player and auto-attacks nearby enemies.

GOBLIN PET (spawned mid-game):
  A crop-carrying helper you can command.
  Commands (beside the command panel): Follow / Stay / Go Home.

================================================================================
                          TRAVEL & WAGONS
================================================================================

FAST TRAVEL (road signs):
  Six road signs are placed along the roads at Trang Trai / Chua Lang /
  Khu Cho / Hop Dem & Nha Hang / Dinh Phu Ong / Vo Dai Quy Vuong. Stand by a
  sign and press E to open the numbered fast-travel panel, then choose a

HORSES & WAGONS:
  The Night Club has a parked delivery wagon with a horse (dressed scene).

CLUB DELIVERY WAGON (interactive scenery):
  The club's wagon can be ridden like a vehicle from its seat (sit + steer via
  movement keys; movement is gated by NavGrid walkability).

================================================================================
                          QUESTS & STORY
================================================================================

MAIN QUESTS:
  1. HARVEST WHEAT - collect 100 wheat. Reward 250 gold.
  2. SLAY MONSTERS - defeat 30 enemies. Reward 500 gold.
  3. EARN COINS   - accumulate 100,000 gold. Reward 1,000 gold.
  Completing all three unlocks the HAPPY ENDING cutscene.

DAILY QUESTS (Jessica):
  Each morning Jessica gives a daily task (fish 3 today, deliver a resource,
  water plants, etc.). Complete it before 6 AM or the offer resets. Rewards
  include affection (+hearts) and Karma (Max Karma) increases.

STORY (Jessica / wedding):
  Build the mansion to hold the wedding. Jessica hands you a fishing rod to
  catch 3 fish for her first quest, then later asks you to build the mansion.
  Completing her requests grants affection and leads to marriage.

BATTLES AT NIGHT:
  From ~6 PM to 6 AM demons appear and attack buildings and villagers. Use the
  ROSARY for one-hit holy kills, close doors at night, and keep fences/walls
  to block them.

KARMA / MAX KARMA:
  Acts of virtue and quests raise Karma and Max Karma (used by the praying
  minigame to grow). Higher Max Karma strengthens the player.

TWO ENDINGS:
  HAPPY ENDING - all main quests complete; walk to the end of the road with
    Teto. Press Enter to keep playing.
  SAD ENDING   - a wagon with a groom leaves; the "KET THUC DAU BUONG" screen.

================================================================================
                          TECHNICAL NOTES
================================================================================

  - All UI is code-generated (no prefabs for HUD or menus)
  - Unity New Input System (Keyboard.current, Mouse.current)
  - CharacterController for player movement
  - URP (Universal Render Pipeline) for rendering
  - Runtime-generated world: trees (~375), rocks (~120), crops and buildings;
    static geometry (rocks, border, fields, crops) is renderer-combined into
    single meshes to cut draw calls; a gated world-build stats log helps
    measure renderer/material counts
  - Building preview: translucent ghost cube follows crosshair
  - Building menu: ScrollRect with VerticalLayoutGroup for scrollable list
  - Inventory HUD: always visible at bottom of screen
  - Stats HUD: always visible at top-left of screen
  - Fast travel : world space road signs + a numbered panel
  - Night Club : dynamic day/night toggle (controller)
